Poker Dice Dash brings Texas-style poker, dice and Blackjack 21 into one app, so you can play alone against bots or with your friends.

Game modes:
• Texas Hold'em with dice: a variant with random events that shake up every hand.
• Classic Texas Hold'em, no dice.
• Blackjack 21 with Hit, Stand, Double, Split and the 5-Card Charlie rule.

Local multiplayer:
• Create a room and connect phones and PCs on the same Wi-Fi network.
• No external servers, no accounts and no ads.
• Automatic reconnection if the signal drops for a moment.
