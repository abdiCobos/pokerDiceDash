Poker Dice Dash is a pocket casino: poker with dice, classic Texas Hold'em and Blackjack in one app. Play alone against bots or with friends on the same Wi-Fi network, with no accounts, no ads and no internet needed.

<b>Dice Dash: poker with dice</b>
Before every move you roll two dice, and some rolls shake up the hand:
• 1 + 1, Swap: everybody's hole cards are pooled and dealt again.
• 6 + 6, All-in: the roller pushes all their chips.
• 3 + 3, Spy: you see one opponent card.
• 6 + 1, Extra card: you get a third card and keep the best two.
• Total 7, Lucky dice: the pot grows by 30% (once per hand).

<b>Texas Hold'em</b>
Classic poker with blinds, side pots and the full rule set.

<b>Blackjack</b>
Hit, stand, double or split. Blackjack pays 3 to 2, the dealer stands on 17, 5-Card Charlie rule and insurance when the dealer shows an ace.

<b>Bots with personality</b>
Opponents at three levels (easy, normal and hard) that weigh their odds, bluff and play differently from each other.

<b>Multiplayer on your local network</b>
• Create a room and join from other phones or Windows PCs on the same Wi-Fi.
• Rooms show up on their own; you can also join by IP.
• Poker and Blackjack over the network, with an optional password and automatic reconnection.

No external servers, no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

The app is in Spanish.
